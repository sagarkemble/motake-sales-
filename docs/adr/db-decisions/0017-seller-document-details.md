# Seller PAN, bank details, terms and place are nullable Seller master columns read when a PDF is drawn

Spec J's redrawn Documents (#45, "PDF redesign") print the seller's PAN, the company bank account, Terms and Conditions and a place ("Received At" on a Payment receipt, "Place" on a Note). None of these may be hard-coded, so `seller_settings` gains eight nullable columns: `pan` varchar(10), `bank_account_name` varchar(200), `bank_name` varchar(120), `bank_branch` varchar(120), `bank_account_number` varchar(34), `bank_ifsc` varchar(11), `terms` text (one term per line) and `place` varchar(120). Existing rows keep working with all of them empty, and a paper leaves out the PAN, the bank block or the terms while their fields are empty. They are edited and audited like the other seller fields (`updated` audit row with old and new values); PAN and IFSC are checked against their formats and stored upper-cased. A GSTIN's characters 3–12 are the PAN it was issued to, so when both are set the PAN must match those characters of the seller GSTIN; a mismatch is refused.

They are not added to the Bill letterhead stamp or to `notes`. A Document is drawn once and its PDF is kept under `pdf_key`, so what it printed is already frozen; the next draw (a new Bill version, a Correct IRN redraw, a new Note or receipt) uses the Seller master of that moment, the same way the seller phone, email and the whole Payment receipt seller already work (db-decisions/0014). The statutory seller stamp (legal name, address, GSTIN) stays as it is. Migration `0006_seller_document_details`.

**Considered:** stamping the new fields into `bills.letterhead` and `notes` (a JSON and schema change for display-only fields whose PDF is stored anyway); a separate `seller_bank_accounts` table (one account is printed; a table adds a choice nobody asked for); constants in the PDF module (the issue forbids hard-coded seller details).
